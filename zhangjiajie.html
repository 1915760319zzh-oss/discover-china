Zhangjiajie

张家界 - Avatar Mountains & Glass Bridge in the Clouds

Overview

Zhangjiajie National Forest Park is famous for its towering sandstone pillars that inspired the floating mountains in the movie Avatar. Located in Hunan Province, the park features over 3,000 quartzite columns, some rising over 1,000 meters. The surreal landscape, combined with the world's longest and highest glass bridge, makes Zhangjiajie one of China's most spectacular destinations.

Quick Facts

  • Location: Zhangjiajie City, Hunan Province
  • Park Size: 481 square kilometers
  • UNESCO Status: Global Geopark since 2004
  • Best Time to Visit: April-October (avoid rainy season)
  • Entrance Fee: ¥228 (valid for 4 days)
  • Opening Hours: 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM (varies by season)

🚗 How to Get There - Transportation

Fly to Zhangjiajie

Zhangjiajie Hehua Airport: Direct flights from Beijing (2.5h), Shanghai (2.5h), Guangzhou (2h). Airport to city: ¥30, 30 min.

High-Speed Train

From Changsha: 2-2.5 hours, ¥150-200. Zhangjiajie West Railway Station is 10 km from city center.

To National Forest Park

From City: Bus from Zhangjiajie Central Bus Station to Wulingyuan (40 min, ¥13). Then park entrance shuttle.

Within the Park

Park Shuttle Buses: Free with entrance ticket. Connect all major scenic areas. Runs 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM.

💡 Essential Travel Tips

  • Allow 2-3 Days: The park is huge - don't try to see everything in one day
  • Best Time: April-October; September-October for clearest skies and autumn colors
  • Early Morning: Arrive at 7 AM opening to avoid crowds and get best photos
  • Weather: Misty/rainy days create magical atmosphere but reduce visibility
  • What to Bring: Comfortable hiking shoes, rain jacket, water, snacks, camera
  • Physical Fitness: Some areas require hiking; cable cars/elevators available for most spots
  • Altitude: Not extremely high (1,000-1,300m) but bring layers - cooler at top
  • Photography: Best light early morning; mist creates dramatic effects
  • Payment: WeChat Pay and Alipay accepted; carry some cash for small vendors
  • Combine with: Tianmen Mountain (separate ticket, ¥258) for full Zhangjiajie experience
