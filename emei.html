Emei Mountain

峨眉山 - Sacred Buddhist Peak with Golden Summit

Overview

Mount Emei is one of China's four sacred Buddhist mountains and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Located in Sichuan Province, it rises to 3,099 meters at its peak (Golden Summit). The mountain is famous for its stunning sunrise above the clouds, ancient temples, diverse wildlife (especially Tibetan macaques), and rich Buddhist culture. With over 100 temples and monasteries dating back 2,000 years, Emei is a living center of Chinese Buddhism. The hike through lush forests, misty peaks, and stone steps is both physically challenging and spiritually rewarding.

Quick Facts

  • Location: Emeishan City, Sichuan Province
  • UNESCO Status: World Heritage Site since 1996
  • Highest Point: Golden Summit (3,099 meters)
  • Best Time to Visit: Spring (Apr-May) and Autumn (Sep-Oct)
  • Entrance Fee: ¥160 (peak season), ¥110 (off-season)
  • Opening Hours: 6:00 AM - 7:00 PM (varies by season)

🚗 How to Get There - Transportation

By Train to Emei Station

From Chengdu: High-speed train (1.5h, ¥65). Frequent departures. Emei Station is 30 min from mountain entrance by taxi (¥30-40).

By Bus

From Chengdu: Bus from Xinnanmen Bus Station (3-4h, ¥80-100). Arrives at Emei Bus Station, then taxi or local bus to mountain.

To Mountain Entrance

From Emei City: Taxi to Baoguo Temple area (main entrance, 30 min, ¥30-40). Or bus #5 from city center (¥2).

Up the Mountain

Cable Cars: Two cable cars: Wannian (¥65 up) and Golden Summit (¥55 up). Or hike 2-3 days via ancient trails.

️ Recommended Tour Routes

Route 1: Express Day Trip (1 Day)

Route: Baoguo Temple → Bus to Leidongping → Cable car to Golden Summit → Return same way
Duration: 8-10 hours
Best for: Short on time, want to see summit

Route 2: Classic Two-Day Hike

Day 1: Baoguo → Qingyin Pavilion → Wannian Temple → Stay at Hongchunping
Day 2: Hongchunping → Leidongping → Golden Summit (sunrise) → Cable car down

Route 3: Complete Three-Day Trek

Day 1: Baoguo → Fuhu Temple → Qingyin Pavilion → Hongchunping
Day 2: Hongchunping → Xixian Pool → Leidongping → Jieyin Hall
Day 3: Leidongping → Golden Summit (sunrise) → descent

Route 4: Temple & Culture Tour

Focus: Baoguo Temple, Fuhu Temple, Wannian Temple, Golden Summit Temple
Activities: Buddhist ceremonies, Meditation sessions, Monk conversations
Duration: 2-3 days

💡 Essential Travel Tips

  • Physical Fitness: Hiking is strenuous (many stairs). Cable cars available but still lots of walking. Train for stairs before trip.
  • Altitude: Golden Summit at 3,099m. Some feel mild altitude sickness. Ascend slowly, stay hydrated.
  • Monkeys: Tibetan macaques are common. Don't feed them, don't show food, keep bags closed. They can be aggressive.
  • Weather: Mountain weather changes rapidly. Bring rain gear, warm layers (cold at summit even in summer). Check forecast.
  • Best Season: Spring (Apr-May) for flowers, Autumn (Sep-Oct) for clear views. Summer is rainy; winter is cold but snowy.
  • Sunrise: Golden Summit sunrise is spectacular but weather-dependent. Stay overnight for best chance.
  • Temple Etiquette: Remove hats in temples, don't point at Buddha statues, speak quietly, ask before photographing monks.
  • What to Bring: Rain poncho, warm jacket, comfortable hiking shoes, water, snacks, flashlight (for early morning hikes).
  • Crowds: Avoid Chinese holidays (Oct 1-7, May 1-5). Weekdays much quieter than weekends.
  • Payment: WeChat Pay and Alipay accepted everywhere. Some temples accept cash donations only.
