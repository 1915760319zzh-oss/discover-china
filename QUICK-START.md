# Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Deploy Your Website in 5 Minutes

### Step 1: Go to Netlify Drop
Open your browser and go to: **https://app.netlify.com/drop**

### Step 2: Upload Your Website
You have two options:

**Option A: Drag & Drop Folder**
1. Open File Explorer
2. Navigate to: `C:\Users\888\Desktop\china-travel-website`
3. Drag the entire folder to the Netlify drop zone

**Option B: Upload Zip File**
1. Click "choose a file" on Netlify
2. Select: `C:\Users\888\Desktop\china-travel-website-final.zip`
3. Wait for upload to complete

### Step 3: Get Your Live URL
Netlify will give you a URL like:
```
https://random-name-12345.netlify.app
```

### Step 4: Share Your Website!
Your website is now live and accessible worldwide!
