Kanas Lake

喀纳斯 - Mysterious Color-Changing Lake in Altai Mountains

Overview

Kanas Lake is a stunning alpine lake in the Altai Mountains of northern Xinjiang, near the borders of Russia, Kazakhstan, and Mongolia. The lake is famous for its color-changing waters — shifting from blue to green to silver depending on the weather and season — and local legends of a mysterious lake monster. Surrounded by dense taiga forests, snow-capped peaks, and Tuva Mongolian villages, Kanas is often called "God's Backyard Garden." The area is home to the Tuva people, an ethnic minority with unique culture and traditions. Autumn (late September to early October) is the most spectacular season when the forests turn brilliant gold and red.

Quick Facts

  • Location: Burqin County, Altai Prefecture, Xinjiang
  • Lake Depth: 188.5 meters (deepest in northwest China)
  • Altitude: 1,374 meters above sea level
  • Best Time to Visit: Late September - early October (autumn colors)
  • Entrance Fee: ¥185 (includes park bus) + ¥80 (observation platform)
  • Opening Hours: 8:00 AM - 7:00 PM (peak season)

🚗 How to Get There - Transportation

By Air + Car

Fly to Burqin: Kanas Airport (seasonal, Jun-Oct). Flights from Urumqi (1h, ¥800-1200). Airport to Kanas: 2h drive (taxi ¥300-400).

By Air to Altay

Altay Airport: Flights from Urumqi (1h, ¥600-900). Then drive to Burqin (2h) and Kanas (3h more). Total 5h drive.

By Car from Urumqi

Distance: 700km (10-12h drive). Route: Urumqi → Karamay → Burqin → Kanas. Mountain roads, some rough sections. 4WD recommended.

Within Scenic Area

Park Bus: Included in entrance fee. Runs between all major stops (Kanas Lake, Moon Bay, Dragon Bay, Fairy Bay). Private cars not allowed inside.

Day 3: Return via different trail → Three bays

💡 Essential Travel Tips

  • Best Season: Late September - early October for autumn colors (ABSOLUTE PEAK). June-August for green forests. Winter (Nov-Mar) extremely cold and road closures.
  • Book Early: Peak autumn season books out months ahead. Reserve hotels and flights early.
  • Remote Location: Kanas is very remote. Bring medications, snacks, and supplies. Limited shops inside park.
  • Weather: Mountain weather changes rapidly. Bring warm layers even in summer. Snow possible in autumn.
  • Altitude: Lake at 1,374m - generally no altitude issues. Some hiking trails are strenuous.
  • Permits: Border area near Russia/Kazakhstan/Mongolia. Carry passport/ID at all times. Check current regulations.
  • Photography: Best light early morning and late afternoon. Autumn colors peak late September.
  • Tuva Culture: Respect local customs. Ask before photographing people. Some Tuva families welcome visitors.
  • Transport: Private cars not allowed inside park. Use park bus system (included in ticket).
  • Payment: WeChat Pay and Alipay accepted. ATMs limited - bring cash from Burqin or Urumqi.
