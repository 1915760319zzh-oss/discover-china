Lijiang Old Town

丽江古城 - Naxi Culture and Cobblestone Streets

Overview

Lijiang Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site nestled in the mountains of Yunnan Province. With over 800 years of history, it showcases the unique architecture and culture of the Naxi people. The town's cobblestone streets, ancient water channels, and ornate wooden buildings create a fairy-tale atmosphere. Surrounded by snow-capped mountains and lush valleys, Lijiang has been a major trading post on the ancient Tea Horse Road for centuries.

Quick Facts

  • Location: Lijiang City, Yunnan Province
  • UNESCO Status: World Heritage Site since 1997
  • Altitude: 2,400 meters (acclimatize slowly)
  • Best Time to Visit: March-May and September-November
  • Old Town Maintenance Fee: ¥50 (valid for 1 year)
  • Opening Hours: Open 24 hours (shops typically 9 AM - 10 PM)

🚗 How to Get There - Transportation

By Air

Lijiang Sanyi Airport: Flights from Kunming (1h, ¥400), Chengdu (1.5h, ¥600), Beijing (3.5h, ¥1200). Airport to Old Town: taxi ¥80-100, bus ¥20.

By Train

From Kunming: High-speed train (3.5h, ¥220). From Chengdu: Overnight train (10h, ¥250 sleeper). Lijiang Station is 30 min from Old Town by taxi (¥30).

By Bus

From Kunming: Long-distance bus (8-10h, ¥200). From Dali: Bus (3h, ¥80). Buses arrive at Lijiang Bus Station, walk or take taxi to Old Town.

Within Old Town

Walking only: No cars allowed inside Old Town. Luggage carts available. For Jade Dragon Snow Mountain: bus from town (¥15) or tour (¥200-300).

💡 Essential Travel Tips

  • Altitude: Lijiang is at 2,400m - take it easy first day, drink water, avoid alcohol. Some feel mild altitude sickness.
  • Best Season: Spring (Mar-May) and Autumn (Sep-Nov) for mild weather and clear skies. Summer is rainy; winter is cold but less crowded.
  • Crowds: Avoid Chinese national holidays (Oct 1-7, May 1-5) - extremely crowded and prices double.
  • Maintenance Fee: ¥50 Old Town fee is required; usually included in hotel booking. Keep receipt.
  • Bargaining: Expected in markets and small shops. Start at 50% of asking price.
  • Safety: Old Town is very safe, but watch for pickpockets in crowded areas.
  • Dress: Layers essential - cool mornings/evenings, warm afternoons. Comfortable shoes for cobblestones.
  • Photography: Ask permission before photographing Naxi people. Some may expect small tip (¥5-10).
  • Naxi Culture: Respect local customs. Don't touch religious artifacts. Remove shoes when entering homes.
  • Payment: WeChat Pay and Alipay widely accepted. ATMs available. Cash needed for small vendors.
